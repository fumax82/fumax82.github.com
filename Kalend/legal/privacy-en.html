Privacy Policy

In short

Kalend collects no data about you: no account, no advertising, no tracking, no usage statistics, no servers of ours. Everything you enter stays on your device. Some features, if you use them, send the minimum necessary to third-party services, described below.

Data that stays on the device

Events, tasks, chains, lists, tags, settings, automatic backups, places you have searched, personal keys and links of connected calendars (the last two encrypted with a key that never leaves the phone).

What leaves the phone and to whom

Only when you use the feature: • Weather: coordinates rounded to about 1 km go to the weather service you chose (MET Norway, Norway, by default); the provider sees your IP address like any server you contact. • Places: the text of the place you search goes to Photon (komoot, Germany) on OpenStreetMap data, only if the atlas on the phone does not know it. • Services with your own key (Open‑Meteo, OpenWeatherMap): coordinates or place text together with your key, under the policy of the provider you chose. • Connected calendars: the link you entered is downloaded from its operator (Google, Microsoft, Apple or others). • "Open in Google Calendar": the data of the event you choose to open goes to Google. • Dictation: the system's speech recognition runs on the phone when available; otherwise the system's voice service may send audio to its provider's servers (for example Google) under its own policy. Kalend does not record or keep audio. • QR sync: data travels directly between your devices on the local network, with no server.

Permissions

Location (optional, only while the app is open): for sky, sunrise, sunset and weather where you are. Microphone (optional): to dictate events. Phone calendar (optional): to read those events next to yours; Kalend does not modify them. Notifications and exact alarms: for reminders at the time you choose. Every permission can be denied or revoked in the system settings.

Backups

Android may include the app's data in the system backup (Google Drive), encrypted with your screen lock and managed by Google under its own policy; personal keys and encrypted links are excluded. Manual exports, plain or password-protected, are decided and kept by you.

Retention and deletion

Data stays as long as you keep the app. You can delete everything from Settings → Data or by uninstalling the app. There are no copies with us.

Children

The app is not intended for children under 13 (or the age set by local law) and collects no data in any case.

Your rights

Data protection laws (for example the GDPR) grant you access, rectification, erasure, portability and the right to complain to a supervisory authority. Since the data lives only on your device, you exercise these rights directly there; for third-party services, contact their providers. Any question: [indirizzo e-mail di contatto].
